# Ages of Calradia v1.5.12 combined patch

This patch preserves the user-approved v1.5.11 political renderer exactly and
updates only the guarded compatibility sidecar.

## Corrected systems

- tournament start and expiration cadence;
- effective troop-wage labels and wage explanations;
- clan income, expenses, workshop income, mercenary finance, and wallet
  changes are annualized once instead of being divided twice;
- workshop base production is scaled before Bannerlord applies perks,
  policies, buildings, and other modifiers;
- town food counts the physical market contribution, while direct food
  production and consumption are scaled exactly once;
- campaign-time synchronization preserves native siege Engineering, perk, and
  construction calculations;
- war proposals use the annual-equivalent 87-day truce instead of the longer
  over-scaled cooldown;
- scene popup dates use the Gregorian formatter;
- the existing save-age compatibility path remains active for saves created
  with or without the mod;
- town/city labels remain readable on the campaign and strategic maps.

## Compatibility and safety

- Target game: Bannerlord v1.4.8.
- The main `AgesOfCalradia.dll` is the immutable `560F1B51...` approved build.
- The combined sidecar validates that exact DLL and all required native targets
  before registering patches. If validation fails, it disables itself safely.
- No diagnostics module, diagnostic log, PDB, or test-only asset ships in this
  release.
