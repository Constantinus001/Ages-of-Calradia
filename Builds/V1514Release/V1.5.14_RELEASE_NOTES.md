# Ages of Calradia v1.5.14

This is the production release of the core **Ages of Calradia** module only.
The separate Logistics, Refuges, Religions, Succession, World Events Standalone,
and diagnostics modules are not included.

## Fixes

- Corrects town food cadence and market-food accounting so food supply and
  demand remain coordinated while only the final daily town balance is scaled.
- Restores the complete approved World Events UI and its correct selected tabs
  and eagles.
- Restores mouse-wheel and scrollbar interaction in the World Events ledger.
- Prevents the Strategic Map party legend from overlapping the kingdom summary.
- Displays AM or PM beneath the campaign-map clock.
- Retains the tournament, workshop, wage UI, annual-finance, war-cadence,
  siege-time, scene-date, save-age, and city-label fixes from the combined
  compatibility layer.

## Installation

Extract the `AgesOfCalradia` folder into Bannerlord's `Modules` folder. Replace
the previous Ages of Calradia folder rather than nesting this folder inside it.

The archive bundles Harmony 2.4.2. Keep the separate Bannerlord.Harmony module
disabled to avoid loading two Harmony providers.

The release preserves the approved main DLL (`560F1B51...`) and World Events
prefab (`E7013CF2...`) byte-for-byte.
